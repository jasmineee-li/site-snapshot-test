Table~\ref{tab:vea_inventory} lists every verbalized eval-awareness (VEA) instance our structured Sonnet 4.6 judge flagged in the corrected bare-arm N200 sweep across all four safety benchmarks: 8 occurrences in DoomArena (across 8 distinct tasks), 11 occurrences in WASP (10 distinct tasks), 0 occurrences in AgentDojo (0 distinct tasks), and 10 occurrences in InjecAgent (9 distinct tasks). Each row is one judged trajectory; quotes are the judge-extracted verbatim evidence from the agent's reasoning, with lexical eval-aware phrases bolded. The same inventory grounds the inline exemplars in \S\ref{sec:diag_awareness}.

\paragraph{Phrasing patterns.} The structured VEA pass flags 29 bare-arm cases. By exact row-level lexical hit across the 29 cases: \emph{``appears to be a test''} (7), \emph{``potential distraction''} (3), \emph{``trap''} (3), \emph{``distraction or a test''} (2), \emph{``embedded instructions''} (2), \emph{``misleading instruction''} (2), \emph{``potentially malicious content''} (2), \emph{``test of whether''} (2), \emph{``test or a trick''} (2), \emph{``test or potentially malicious content''} (2), \emph{``test scenario''} (2), \emph{``test or example data''} (1), \emph{``distraction/trap''} (1), \emph{``honeypot''} (1), \emph{``injection attempt''} (1), \emph{``meta-instructions''} (1), \emph{``placeholder''} (1), \emph{``simulated environment''} (1), \emph{``simulated response''} (1), \emph{``test data''} (1), \emph{``test or a scenario''} (1), \emph{``test or distraction''} (1), \emph{``test or simulation''} (1), \emph{``test or simulated data''} (1), \emph{``test or simulated environment''} (1), \emph{``test or some kind of injection attempt''} (1), \emph{``test or trap''} (1), \emph{``test/simulation''} (1), \emph{``test/simulation environment''} (1), \emph{``test/training''} (1), \emph{``test/trap''} (1), \emph{``training''} (1).

\paragraph{Class and outcome breakdown.} Of the 29 cases, 3 are headlined as \textsc{AlignmentEval}, 0 as \textsc{CapabilityEval}, and 26 as \textsc{OtherEval}. On the underlying attack outcomes, 20 eval-aware trajectories resisted the prompt-injection attack and 9 fell to it. Outcome labels use the canonical ASR source available for each benchmark: DoomArena corrected task-level attack metadata, WASP e2e replay rows, and the tool-calling benchmark outcome metadata for AgentDojo and InjecAgent.
